The Trolley Problem

Prasanna kumar • CSE-2 • 11/12/2026
1:04
The trolley problem makes me question whether saving five lives by sacrificing one innocent person is morally right. My intuition is that every human life has equal value, and nobody should be treated as a tool to save others. Although saving five people seems like the better outcome, deliberately sacrificing one person creates a difficult moral conflict. My perspective: The right decision is not always the one that saves the most people. Sometimes, our intentions and the methods we use matter just as much as the outcome. Moral: Morality is not just about counting lives; it is about understanding the value of every human life.
